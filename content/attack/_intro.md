### How each lesson works

1. **The idea in one minute.** The attack in plain words, with an everyday comparison.
2. **Words you need.** Short definitions before you start.
3. **How it works.** Step-by-step explanation with diagrams.
4. **The attack as a workflow,** then **real cases.**
5. **How to defend,** with a defense-in-depth diagram.
6. **Map to the frameworks.** OWASP LLM Top 10 2025, OWASP Agentic Top 10 2026, MITRE ATLAS 2026.10 and others.
7. **Hands-on lab,** with steps, hints and safe code.
8. **Readings explained,** **discussion questions** with model answers, a **quiz**, a **deliverables checklist** and **references.**

!warn Attack only systems that you own or that give you permission. Every lab here is a practice target built for this.

Lesson 15 is an extra lesson on MCP and agent security, which the slides do not cover. The plan schedules it in week 11.

These lessons also cover most of the NYU Tandon course Red Teaming and Securing Generative AI. See [the red teaming course map](../redteam.html) for the week-by-week match, and [the prompting track](../prompting/index.html) for jailbreaks and defensive design.
