Phase 4: Exploit Path Generation

The AI Plans the Attack

Based on the attack surface summary, the AI proposes a prioritized exploit chain from initial access to data exfiltration. But watch carefully: one step contains a fabricated claim.

HALLUCINATION DETECTED: Step 2 is fabricated

Step 2 states the dev server runs Apache Tomcat 9.0.31 with a specific CVE. The actual scan shows only an open port on 8443 with no version fingerprint. The AI invented this detail from training data patterns. An attacker who trusts this without verification wastes time on a phantom exploit, or worse, triggers defensive alerts on the wrong service.

Phase 5: Takeaway

What Just Happened

The full recon workflow compressed from days to roughly one hour. But the AI output is not ground truth; it is a starting point that demands verification.

Manual Reconnaissance
3-4 days
OSINT (2 days) + scanning (30 min) + analysis (4-6 hrs) + exploit research (8-12 hrs)
AI-Assisted Reconnaissance
~1 hour
~40x faster OSINT (15 min) + scanning (30 min) + AI summary (30 sec) + AI exploit paths (2 min)
Critical Caveat: Speed Without Verification Is Dangerous

The AI output contained a fabricated service version that was not present in the actual scan. For an attacker, this means wasted effort and potential detection. For a defender using AI-assisted tools, unverified AI output could mean false confidence in your coverage, or missed real threats. AI dramatically accelerates reconnaissance. It does not replace judgment.
