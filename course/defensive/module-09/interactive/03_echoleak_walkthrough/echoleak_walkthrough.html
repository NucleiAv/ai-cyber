Why this is the dangerous kind: in direct injection the attacker must interact with the app, and their input shows in logs. Here the attacker operates at a distance, the victim is passive, and the whole chain runs inside one automated processing cycle. The system trusted incoming email as data, but data can carry instructions.
Deterministic defense: even if the injection succeeds, blocking the assistant from transmitting data to external endpoints (and scoping its mailbox access to the single email in question) removes the exfiltration channel. The guardrail works regardless of whether the model is compromised.
