Deploying Under Known Risk

Security leads make deployment decisions under uncertainty every day. This exercise puts you in that role: read the scenario, make a call, and select your mitigations. There is no single correct answer, but some positions are easier to defend than others.

Scenario

You are the security lead at a mid-size financial services firm. Your product team wants to deploy an LLM-powered internal assistant that processes employee queries against company documents (a RAG-based system). The assistant would have read access to HR policies, financial reports, and client communications.

The product team argues that competitors already have similar systems and that the productivity gains justify the risk.

Your security assessment found:

  • Prompt injection against the RAG pipeline is architecturally possible; no defense fully prevents it.
  • Indirect injection via poisoned documents is a realistic threat vector.
  • The system would have access to sensitive client data.
Step 1
What is your recommendation?
Step 2
Which mitigations would you require? Select all that apply.
There is no single correct answer here. This analysis represents one possible recommended outcome. Every situation is different: business pressures vary, regulatory environments differ, and the sensitivity and type of data an organization works with is not consistent even within the same industry. The skill is reasoning through the trade-offs with the frameworks you have, not arriving at a predetermined conclusion.
