Setup for the Hugging Face courses

You need no local install. Everything runs in the browser with a free Hugging Face account. The Agents Course has notebooks that open in Colab, quizzes, and a graded final project on a GAIA benchmark subset. Certificates are free. Notebooks call models through the Hugging Face Inference API, and the free tier has limited credits. Install Ollama only when the phase 3 and 4 labs need it.
