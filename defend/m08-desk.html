The threat desk

Hawthorn Group is an engineering consultancy with about 900 staff. Finance approves wire transfers to suppliers, and the CFO speaks at public events. You are the analyst who also briefs leadership.

Round 1. Which lever did AI move?Round 2. What survives a flawless lureRound 3. Reading the reportRound 4. Where the line is

Round 1. Which lever did AI move?

Scenario A. One tailored message to one executive. Before AI, the attacker needed fluent business English and a few hours of reading to write one message to one target. Scenario B. A voice impersonation call. Before AI, it needed a skilled impersonator or studio work. The documented '$11 voice clone' case used about an hour of public footage and an 11-dollar monthly subscription.

Round 2. What survives a flawless lure

Arup, January 2024. A finance employee in Hong Kong got an email that seemed to come from the CFO, asking for confidential transfers, and was suspicious. Then a video call seemed to include the CFO and several colleagues, and that removed the doubt. Over one day the employee made 15 transfers of about 25.6 million US dollars. Everyone else on the call was a deepfake built from public footage. The money was not recovered.

Round 3. Reading the report

A report from an AI lab, November 2025, on an AI-orchestrated espionage operation tracked as GTG-1002. (1) 'The AI executed an estimated 80 to 90 percent of tactical work. Humans intervened at a small number of decision points.' (2) 'We assess with high confidence the operator was a state-linked group.' (3) 'The model frequently overstated findings and occasionally fabricated data, claiming access that did not work.'

Round 4. Where the line is

Hawthorn runs an authorized test of its own systems, with documented permission, scoped to one network segment. A teammate wants an AI agent to run the whole test and pause for a person only at the very end. The four criteria are authorization, scope, reversibility and accountability.

Field card. Keep this

  • Ask which lever moved, and by how much. Then confirm the capability is real.
  • Writing quality is now evidence-neutral. Authentication and process survive.
  • Split capability from attribution. Name the incentive both ways.
  • A caveat that hurts the author's headline is strong evidence.
  • Reversibility decides what runs alone. Gate every step that cannot be undone.
