The frameworks behind AI security

Security teams share a few maps. Each page here explains one of them from zero, shows it step by step, says how to use it, and links it to the others.

Why several maps

No single framework covers everything. Each answers a different question.

  • MITRE ATLAS. What do attackers do to AI systems?
  • MITRE ATT&CK. What do attackers do to computers and networks?
  • MITRE D3FEND. What can defenders do, and which attack does each defense counter?
  • STRIDE. What can go wrong in my design?
  • OWASP LLM and Agentic Top 10. Which risks do AI apps most often get wrong?
  • ARTEX. How does a real autonomous attack agent work, and which brakes keep it safe?
Key point

Use them together. Threat model the design with STRIDE, check it against OWASP, name attacker behavior with ATLAS and ATT&CK, and pick defenses with D3FEND.

Each page has an animated walk-through, a plain explanation, a how-to, examples, strengths and limits, a hands-on task, a practice desk and a quiz.
